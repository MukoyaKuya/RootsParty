Cookie Policy

We prefer organic hemp cookies, but digital ones are necessary.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They act as a memory for the website, helping it remember if you've logged in or if you prefer dark mode.

How We Use Them

  • ● Essential cookies: Required for the site to work.
  • ● Analytics cookies: To understand our traffic (Google Analytics).
  • ● Functional cookies: To remember your choices.

Managing Cookies

You can delete cookies as you wish using your browser settings. You can delete all cookies that are already on your computer and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed.

Note: If you do this, you may have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit a site and some services and functionalities may not work.
